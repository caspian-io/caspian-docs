---
title: "Devices"
description: "How Caspian shows company devices and their posture."
---

Devices lists the devices Caspian detects, with owner, platform and posture. They come from connected device management tools such as Kandji, JumpCloud, SimpleMDM and Fleet, and from the Caspian endpoint agent.

On the Devices page you can:

- see each device's owner and platform;
- check whether devices meet the posture policies set for the workspace;
- see policy violations and notify the device owner.

Employees see their own laptop under Home, with the checks it needs to pass and a step to fix each one.
