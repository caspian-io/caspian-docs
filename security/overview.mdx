---
title: "Security and data"
description: "Where Caspian keeps data and how it is handled."
---

Caspian runs on Amazon Web Services in the EU. The production environment and its database are in Frankfurt (eu-central-1), and backups are copied to Ireland (eu-west-1).

AI features call Anthropic's Claude models through Amazon Bedrock, using EU inference profiles only. Caspian does not train or fine-tune any AI model with customer data.

Caspian runs ISO 27001 and SOC 2 continuously in Vanta. The ISO 27001 Stage 1 audit cleared on 7 October 2026, and the SOC 2 Type II observation period runs until 30 November 2026. An independent penetration test is complete, and the report is available on request.

For current certifications, the list of subprocessors and other documents, see the [Trust Center](https://trust.getcaspian.io). The data processing agreement is at [getcaspian.io/dpa](https://getcaspian.io/dpa).

Security questions: support@getcaspian.io.
