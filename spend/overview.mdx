---
title: "Spend"
description: "How Caspian shows software spend, invoices and vendors."
---

Caspian reads invoices, licence costs and contracts so IT can see what the company pays for software next to who uses it. Accounting integrations such as QuickBooks and Xero can be connected from Integrations.

The Vendors report under Reports lists every vendor the company uses, with its apps, desktop software and cloud accounts rolled up. Each app also has its own page under Apps.
