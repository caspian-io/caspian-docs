---
title: "Access"
description: "How Caspian shows and manages who has which app."
---

Access shows who gets which app and lets IT manage it in one place. People are grouped by department, directory group, user or HR classification, and each group is given apps. A person receives every app any of their groups gives, at the highest role.

The Access page has three tabs:

- **Setup:** the access matrix of groups against apps, where the access rules live. Changes are applied as a run, which is listed in Activity.
- **Requests:** what people ask IT, such as an app or admin rights on a device, with approve and deny.
- **Review:** access reviews, in which reviewers keep, change or remove each account. A completed review becomes a report under Reports.

Accounts that fall outside the rules appear in Findings.
