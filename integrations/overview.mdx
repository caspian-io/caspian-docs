---
title: "Integrations overview"
description: "What integrations are in Caspian and which ones exist."
---

Integrations connect Caspian to your identity provider, HR system and SaaS apps, so it can read who has access to what. Open Integrations from the sidebar to see every connected source and to add a new one.

Available integrations, by category:

- **Identity:** 1Password, Auth0
- **HR:** [HiBob](/integrations/hibob), Deel, Greenhouse
- **Business apps:** Asana, Attio, Figma, Google Calendar, Google Chat, Google Drive, HubSpot, Notion, QuickBooks, Slack, Teamleader Orbit, Xero
- **Developer tools:** Confluence, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Vercel
- **Data and observability:** Datadog, Sentry
- **AI:** Claude, Cursor, OpenAI
- **Other:** Azure DevOps, Gemini, Linear, Toggl Track, Trello

Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace and Okta are connected as identity providers; see [Connect your identity provider](/getting-started/connect-identity-provider). Device management sources such as Kandji, JumpCloud, SimpleMDM and Fleet are also connected from Integrations.

What Caspian can do in each app differs. The page for each integration, where one exists, says what it covers.
