---
title: "Connect your identity provider"
description: "Create a workspace and connect Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace or Okta."
---

Caspian starts from your company directory. Sign in at [app.getcaspian.io](https://app.getcaspian.io), create a workspace, and connect the identity provider your company uses. Caspian supports three: Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace and Okta. Each one has a Connect button during setup, and the same choice is available later under Integrations.

## Before you start

Connect with an account that is allowed to grant the access Caspian asks for. Caspian only reads from the provider.

- **Google Workspace:** read access to users, groups and the list of apps used in the company. The Google Admin privileges are Users > Read, Groups > Read, and Reports or Security > User Security Management.
- **Microsoft Entra ID:** admin consent for Directory.Read.All (read users and groups) and Application.Read.All (read apps).
- **Okta:** an API Services app with read-only scopes and the Read-Only Administrator role.

## Okta steps

1. In Caspian, open Integrations, choose Okta and enter your Okta domain, such as acme.okta.com.
2. In the Okta Admin Console, create an API Services app named Caspian.
3. Grant the read scopes listed in Caspian and assign the Read-Only Administrator role.
4. Copy the client ID and client secret into Caspian and choose Connect.

Caspian checks the app at once and starts the first sync. For Google Workspace and Entra ID, choose Connect and approve the consent screen.

If a connection stops working, the provider row shows Reconnect.
