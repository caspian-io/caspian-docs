Caspian is an IT platform at [app.getcaspian.io](https://app.getcaspian.io). It connects to a company's identity provider (Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace or Okta), its SaaS apps, its devices and its invoices. It then shows IT which software, access, devices and spend the company has, and what to do about them.

This help center explains how to connect Caspian and what each part of the app does. Inside the app, the main pages are Home, Findings, Activity, Apps, Devices, Users, Access, Lifecycle, Reports and Integrations.
